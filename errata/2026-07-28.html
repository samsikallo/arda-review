supersededShe sailed south from the wreck of the realm with the youngest son — the corpus never names her.

nowThe corpus never names her, and that clause is exact: across tiers 1 and 2 the only entry for her is an index line, "'Tradition of Isildur'; Isildur's wife." What it does record of her is not what this entry used to say. She did sail — when Sauron took Minas Ithil in S.A. 3429, Isildur 'went with his wife and his sons by ship down the River, and they sailed from the mouths of Anduin seeking Elendil', which the Tale of Years reads as 'sails north from Anduin's Mouths to Elendil in Arnor, with his wife and sons'. But the youngest son was not aboard: 'Valandil son of Isildur' was 'born in Imladris' the year after, in 3430. And the corpus leaves her in the north-east, not the south — 'his wife and his youngest son, Valandil, he had left in Imladris when he went to the war', and Isildur was riding to Imladris, 'for he had left his wife and youngest son there', when he was killed at the Gladden Fields. The southward sailing belongs to a different pair: in the Akallabêth 'Isildur and Anárion were borne away southwards' out of the Downfall — two brothers, and no wife in the sentence.

reasonThe only index entry for her at all is corpus/t1_d_unfinished_tales...:14070-14071 - "'Tradition of Isildur'; Isildur's wife." The rest of the sentence was wrong, and the passage that settles it is corpus/t1_a_the_silmarillion.txt:13330-13331: "his wife and his youngest son, Valandil, he had left in Imladris when he went to the war".
