WHAT IS UNUSUAL ABOUT IT, STATED PLAINLY BECAUSE IT MATTERS.

This is the BARE GPL with NO FONT EXCEPTION. Most freely-licensed fonts carry one — FreeMonoTengwar,
sitting in the same mirror, spells out the GNU Font Embedding Exception in full, and the OFL faces
this archive already publishes have no such question at all. The exception is what ordinarily
prevents a document's use of a font from affecting the document's own licence.

The archive's owner ruled on 12 August 2026 that this font MAY BE SERVED. That is his decision to
make and it is recorded here rather than re-litigated.

WHAT GPL COMPLIANCE ASKS OF US, so that serving it is done properly:
  * this notice travels with the font
  * the licence is named and linked, not merely implied
  * the SOURCE must remain obtainable. The upstream project is the source of record; this archive
    publishes the compiled webfont only and does not hold the FontForge sources.
