ANIRON — Pete Klassen, 2004. "All Rights Reserved."  Contact: thehutt@gmx.net

WHAT THIS FONT IS. A replication of the typeface used in the front and end credits of Peter
Jackson's "The Lord Of The Rings" trilogy, retraced by hand from the SEE DVD because the
resolution was too low to trace mechanically. Missing characters, punctuation, numerals and a
Cyrillic set were drawn from scratch by the designer.

THE LICENCE POSITION, RECORDED PLAINLY BECAUSE THIS ARCHIVE RECORDS THE POSITION OF EVERYTHING
IT SHIPS. Independent font directories state Aniron as PERSONAL USE ONLY, with commercial use
and modification not permitted and a commercial licence available from the designer. The
readme shipped inside the archive says only "All Rights Reserved", and separately encourages
use for both headings and paragraph text.

THE OWNER'S RULING, 19 August 2026, in his own words: "for now the Arda Archive is personal use
only, we haven't published it because it's not nearly done. I can get licence to publish it,
until that use freely Aniron, if you don't find a better and higher quality similar one."

THE FACT HE WAS GIVEN BEFORE RULING, and it is recorded here so nobody has to rediscover it:
the site IS reachable on the public web at https://samsikallo.github.io/arda-archive and was
serving 200s on the day of this ruling. "Not published" describes intent, not reachability.

WHAT WAS DONE TO THE FILES: the two TTFs were converted to WOFF2 with no subsetting and no
glyph removal — 278 glyphs in, 278 out — because subsetting would be modification and the
licence does not permit it. A container change is the least-altering way to put a font on a
page.

HOW TO SWAP IT OUT IN ONE LINE: every Latin rule reads `var(--face-latin)`. Change that one
custom property and the whole archive changes face. It was built that way on purpose, so this
ruling costs one line to revisit rather than 622 pages.
