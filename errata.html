Errata

what this archive used to say, and what it says now

what this isEvery correction this archive can PROVE it made. Each entry prints the reading that was superseded, struck through and marked with the obelus ÷, beside the reading that replaced it — with the date, the dataset, the record inside it, and the commit that made the change.

and what it is notThis register holds 1,410 corrections. It is not a record of everything that changed. 9,905 further real changes stand in this repository’s history with no stated reason — 88% of everything that changed — and they are NOT dressed as errata, because a change whose commit body states no reason cannot be quoted as one. 1,410 of 11,315 is 12%. A register that published only its own numerator would be making the exact claim this archive exists to refuse.

where it comes fromNot one row of it was written. 567 commits touching a published dataset were read, and only a change whose commit body states a reason naming that record is written here. A change that moved the wording without moving the claim is a refinement and takes no mark at all.

how to check oneEvery prior reading is verbatim. git show <sha>^:<dataset> holds the struck reading at the named field, and git show <sha>:<dataset> holds the one beside it. map/errata_check.py re-opens all 1,410 and refuses if a single one does not stand where it says it stands — and it counts what these leaves PRINT against what the register holds, so an entry lost in the split reddens the gate.

the markerOn these leaves [C], [I-H] and [I-M] grade the REASON’s distance from the record, never a claim about Arda: [C] the commit names this record or a token that moved in it; [I-H] it names the field and nothing narrower; [I-M] it states a reason for the commit and this record is one of the values it changed. The prior reading itself is always verbatim — a record whose prior reading could not be recovered is not written at all.

how it is set1,331 corrections and 79 retractions, across 45 datasets and 22 days. 458 name a published page; the rest name a record inside a dataset that no single page owns. The register is dealt onto leaves at BUILD TIME, not in your browser, so the leaf you are on is the leaf in the archive’s history.
