Borders — ribbon interlace

The plait is the base state: two families of 45° strands reflecting off the band edges, crossing over-and-under on a checkerboard. The knot is the same band with break-lines, which is the whole of Bain's method. The key band is Allen's rectilinear family, set on the diagonal.

Integration — the paste-ready CSS driving the prototype's own class vocabulary

This section carries NO styles of its own for the ornament. It sets data-codex-object="on" on the root and loads the ready-to-paste block verbatim; every mark below is that file acting on the proto's classes — .rubric .versal .illum .catch .folio .fill .tail. The two right-hand panels are the refusals: a declared silence and a struck record must show no ornament at all.
