The Ainur

The Valar and the Maiar, and those of them that fell — every entry anchored to the text

Before Arda was made there were the Ainur, the Holy Ones, the offspring of Ilúvatar's thought. Fourteen of them entered the World as the Valar and were bound to it until its end; with them came the Maiar, of whom Tolkien names comparatively few. Eight are held in chief reverence — the Aratar — and one, mightiest in his beginning, unmade himself.

Where the texts disagree they are shown disagreeing. Melian is of Vána and Estë in one place and of Yavanna's people in another; Estë and Nessa were once not Valier at all but "the highest among the Maiar"; the Blue Wizards have four incompatible accounts and no verdict is given here. Two glosses long quoted as Tolkien's are Christopher's editorial additions, and say so.
