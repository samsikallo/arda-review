Volume VII

The Index

Every name the volumes themselves indexed, with the editor's own statement of what it is — and where two editors both wrote one, both are kept
9,416 names, drawn from the indexes 16 volumes wrote for themselves. These are not page-references with a name attached — Christopher Tolkien's indexes carry, in his own words, a short statement concerning each person and place, so most entries here are a definition written by the editor who knew the material best, and for many names it is the only one that exists. Where several volumes describe the same name, every statement is kept and attributed: identical wordings once, a statement wholly contained in a fuller one folded into it, and genuine disagreements side by side. Nothing is paraphrased. 2,994 names appear in two or more of the indexes.
Sorted letter by letter, ignoring spaces, hyphens and apostrophes; accents sort as their plain letters (Éol under E) and are kept as written. A name is filed under the first letter of the form the index itself printed — nothing is re-filed under a surname or a title.
