Thargelion

lordship · Noldor (Fëanorian) · F.A. 7–455

Its bounds

Between Mount Rerir and the rivers, about dark Lake Helevorn; cold uplands facing the Dwarf-road passes.a

Its living

The Dwarf-trade monopoly: 'Caranthir's people... had the profit' of the traffic of Nogrod and Belegost — the recorded toll-taking makes this the most explicitly commercial Elvish realm.b
