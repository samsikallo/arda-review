Tirion upon Túna

city-state · Noldor (first with the Vanyar) · Y.T. →

Its bounds

Set on the green hill Túna in the Calacirya, the only pass of light through the Pelóri; its topmost tower the Mindon Eldaliéva, whose silver lamp shone far into the mists of the sea; the white ways dusted with diamond-dust.a

Its history

Chief city of the Noldor through the Noontide; emptied of nine-tenths of its people in the Flight; under Finarfin it endures, and its host marches at last in the War of Wrath.b

Its governance

Kingship of the Noldor: Finwë; after his death the crown's contention between Fëanor and Fingolfin splits the people; Finarfin rules the remnant 'the remnant of the Noldor in the deep cleft of the mountains'.c
