Angband

dark empire · Morgoth's host (Orcs, Balrogs, dragons, thrall-Men) · Y.T. (rebuilt F.A.) – F.A. 587

Its bounds

The north-western outwork of Utumno's old realm, beneath the reared slag-towers of Thangorodrim; before it Ard-galen, after the Flame Anfauglith, the Gasping Dust; the Gate 150 leagues from Menegroth.a

Its governance

Absolute thrall-tyranny: Sauron its greatest lieutenant (lord of Tol-in-Gaurhoth's werewolf outpost), Gothmog high-captain; captives worked in the pits; spies and 'seeming-free' released thralls a stated instrument of rule.b

Its living

A war-economy of slavery and smithy: 'the thunder of the forges of Morgoth'; breeding of Orc-hosts and dragons its production; tribute demanded of conquered Hithlum through Easterling clients.c
