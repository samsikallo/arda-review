Angmar

sorcerer-kingdom · orcs & evil Men under the Witch-king · T.A. c.1300–1975

Its bounds

Both sides of the Mountains' northern end: Carn Dûm its capital, Mount Gram its raid-base against the Shire's later memory.a

Its governance

Founded solely to destroy the Dúnedain of the North — a state as instrument, its purpose stated by Gandalf's history and fulfilled at the cost of its own existence.b
