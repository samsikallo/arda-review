Isengard

fortress-realm (usurped) · Gondorian ward → Saruman's orcs, wolves and Men · Gondorian from S.A.-end; Saruman's T.A. 2759–3019

Its bounds

The ring-wall of Angrenost at Nan Curunír, the Wizard's Vale, holding Orthanc of unbreakable black stone with its four-horned crown; the Isen dammed and fouled for industry.a

Its governance

The corpus's technocratic tyranny: 'a mind of metal and wheels'; slave-industry in the delvings, half-orc breeding, the Voice as instrument of rule — and its parody-afterlife in the Shire ('Sharkey').b

Its living

Forge-economy fed by felled Fangorn timber — the casus belli of the Ents; leaf and goods bought covertly from the Shire (the Longbottom barrels at the flood).c
