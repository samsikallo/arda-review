Its bounds

The inland sea of Rhûn with its ash-forests and steppes beyond the map's edge — the corpus shows only the western shore of an eastern world.a

Its governance

Wagon-lordships and khagan-like war-confederacies (the Wainriders 'a people, or a confederacy of many peoples'); stirred repeatedly and explicitly by Sauron's emissaries.b
