Hithlum

kingdom (High Kingship) · Noldor & Sindar (Edain in Dor-lómin) · F.A. 1–473 (Elvish rule)

Its bounds

Between Ered Wethrin and Ered Lómin, cool and rain-swept, holding Lake Mithrim; Dor-lómin its southern fief; the fortress Barad Eithel at Sirion's well its eastern door.a

Its history

Seat of the High Kingship of the Noldor in exile through the Long Peace and both great defeats; its fall at the Nirnaeth ends organized Elvish power in the north-west.b

Its governance

High Kingship: Fingolfin, then Fingon; Dor-lómin granted as lordship to the House of Hador — the clearest canonical vassalage among Elves and Men (Dor-lómin was given to Hador).c
