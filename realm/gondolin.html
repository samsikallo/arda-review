Gondolin

hidden city-kingdom · Noldor & Sindar · F.A. 52–510

Its bounds

The hidden vale of Tumladen in the Encircling Mountains, entered only by the Dry River and the Seven Gates; the city on the hill Amon Gwareth; Ulmo's power on Sirion its outer shield.a

Its history

Built in secrecy over two generations of the sun; endures longest of the Noldor realms; falls last of the great kingdoms, by treason from within.b

Its governance

Kingship of Turgon; the Twelve Houses of the Gondothlim (the Kings, the Wing, the Mole, the Swallow, the Heavenly Arch, the Pillar, the Tower of Snow, the Tree, the Golden Flower, the Fountain, the Harp, the Hammer of Wrath) marshal both city and army — the fullest constitutional detail of any Elven city, given in the early Fall of Gondolin.c
