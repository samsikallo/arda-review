Gondor

kingdom · Dúnedain of the South & the Men of the White Mountains · S.A. 3320 – enduring

Its bounds

From Anduin's mouths to the White Mountains and (at height) from the Greyflood to the Harnen and east to Rhûn's sea: Anórien, Ithilien, Lebennin, Lamedon, Lossarnach, Belfalas of the Princes, Anfalas, Pinnath Gelin — the muster-roll of V.1 is the realm's living map.a

Its history

Two millennia of glory and decline told in App A's annal-density: Ship-kings, Wainrider wars, Plague, Kin-strife, the waning of the blood, the Stewards' long guard — 'Gondor waned, and the line of Meneldil son of Anárion failed'.b

Its governance

Sacral monarchy of the Line of Anárion, then the Ruling Stewards 'until the King returns' (Pelendur's precedent fixing both stewardship and the male-line succession-law that refused Arvedui — the corpus's constitutional crisis of record); the Council of Gondor; the Princes of Dol Amroth hereditary under the crown.c
