Imladris (Rivendell)

refuge-lordship · Elves (mixed), wardens of the North · S.A. 1697 – F.o.A.

Its bounds

The hidden valley of the Bruinen's springs under the Misty Mountains' western wall; the Ford its door, the moors of the Ettenmoors its bleak north.a

Its governance

Not a kingdom: Elrond as master and healer, ruling by wisdom — 'his house was perfect, whether you liked food, or sleep, or work, or story-telling, or singing, or just sitting and thinking best'; the Council its instrument of policy, summoning none yet gathering all.b

Its people

The Hall of Fire and the keeping of songs; the fostering of the Dúnedain heirs ('Estel'); the shards of Narsil above the hearth — a state whose treasury is memory.c
