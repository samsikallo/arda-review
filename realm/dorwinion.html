Dorwinion

trade country · (unstated — Men and/or Elves) · Third Age (attested)

Its bounds

By the Sea of Rhûn's north-west (the map tradition); the corpus text gives only the wine and the river-route: of its people and rule, The attached documents do not provide enough information to answer this.a

Its living

Export viticulture strong enough to supply a king's table two rivers away — the North's long-range trade proven in a single barrel-road.b
