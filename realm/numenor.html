Númenor

island empire · Dúnedain · S.A. 32–3319

Its bounds

Shaped like a 'five-pointed star, or pentangle', its 'five large peninsular promontories' about the Mittalmar, with the Meneltarma at its heart; the road ran from 'Rómenna in the east, to the royal city of Armenelos' and thence to Andúnië in the west, and 'the most beautiful of all the havens of Númenor, Eldalondë the Green' lay on the Andustar. UT's 'Description' treats each region in turn — the corpus's fullest phya

Its history

Given as reward, grown to the mightiest realm of Men, corrupted by the fear of death into empire and blasphemy, and drowned utterly: 'Akallabêth, the Downfallen'. Its story is told whole in the Akallabêth, its court-history in UT and the Line of Elros.b

Its governance

Sacral hereditary monarchy: the Sceptre, eldest child succeeding (the law changed by Tar-Aldarion for Ancalimë — the corpus's most explicit constitutional amendment); the Council of the Sceptre; the King's Court at Armenelos; the lords of Andúnië a cadet line and conscience of the realm.c
