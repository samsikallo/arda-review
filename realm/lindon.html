Lindon

kingdom (High Kingship), later havens-lordship · Noldor & Sindar · S.A. 1 – (enduring)

Its bounds

The land west of Ered Luin that survived Beleriand's drowning — Forlindon and Harlindon about the Gulf of Lhûn, Mithlond at its head.a

Its governance

High Kingship of Gil-galad over the Noldor of the West, with Círdan lord of the Havens under and after him; Elrond his herald and vice-gerent (founding Imladris by his commission).b

Its living

Ship-wrighting for the Straight Road: the white ships of the Havens, the realm's whole later purpose.c
