The Gazetteer of Arda

Every named place the archive holds, with what kind of thing it is, which realms contain it, which Ages it belongs to, and what befell it — each with the citation it came from. Natural and made are kept apart at the root of the taxonomy. Two confidences are shown, never averaged: whether the name is Tolkien's, and whether a text places it where the map does. Framsburg is high on the first and middling on the second — he supplied the name for the Pauline Baynes map, and no narrative text puts it anywhere.
