Volume I

The Twelve Houses of Gondolin

Turgon's hidden realm of Gondolin was ordered into twelve great houses, each with its own lord, colours and device — the fullest heraldic array Tolkien gave to any single city (The Fall of Gondolin; The Book of Lost Tales II). When the city fell in F.A. 510, these were the banners that went down in its last battle — and two of them, the Fountain of Ecthelion and the Golden Flower of Glorfindel, are remembered for the Balrogs their lords slew in the ruin.

❦ ✦ ❦

House of the King

Turgon

Founder and King of the Hidden City; bore the highest device

House of the Wing

Tuor

Sent by Ulmo; wedded Idril and led the survivors out of the ruin

House of the Golden Flower

Glorfindel

Fell slaying a Balrog at Cirith Thoronath, guarding the fugitives' escape

House of the Fountain

Ecthelion

Slew Gothmog, Lord of Balrogs, at the King's Square, and perished with him

House of the Hammer of Wrath

Rog

Mightiest of the smiths; his folk hated Melkor above all and struck deepest

House of the Heavenly Arch

Egalmoth

A lord of many jewels and a curved sword; came alive out of the Fall

House of the Tree

Galdor

Valiant in the last defence of the city

House of the Swallow

Duilin

Swiftest of all in running and in archery

House of the Harp

Salgant

A lord of song turned craven, who fawned upon Maeglin

House of the Pillar

Penlod

Tallest of his people; lord also of the Tower of Snow

House of the Tower of Snow

Penlod

The white tower-guard, held with the Pillar under one lord

House of the Mole

Maeglin

The King's sister-son; his betrayal opened the city to Morgoth
