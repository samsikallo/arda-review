Volume I

The Marked Sheet

The map Pauline Baynes drew in 1969 and Tolkien marked up for her — her linework traced into the page, his notes transcribed beside it
In 1969 Pauline Baynes was commissioned to draw a poster-map of Middle-earth, and Tolkien marked up a copy for her — additional names to include, and advice on a few points of topography. 34 of his notes and hers are transcribed below. The linework here is 25,805 centrelines traced from her sheet, one path per stroke, drawn in the page rather than shown as a picture — the same way the archive draws Aman. The lettering is not traced. Her hand-lettered place-names are her typography as much as her drawing, and tracing them would have reproduced a second thing to do a first thing's job; only the black linework was taken.
