The Names of Arda

The Eldarin kinds of name — who gave which, at what hour, and which of them counted as true; with the Quenya left out wherever Tolkien left it out
An Elf of the Noldor bore more than one name, and they were not of one kind. The father gave one and the mother might give another; the child chose a third for itself, at a ceremony that could not be held until it was old enough to take pleasure in the sounds of words. Others added by-names over a life. 6 kinds are set out below, and two of them carry no Quenya word at all — because Tolkien named the ceremony and did not name the name. Where the corpus is silent this page is blank, and says so.

What a name is, besides its kind

Kind is not the only thing a name has. Tolkien's own text divides them three ways at once: a name is true or it is not; it is public or private — and private, he is explicit, is not secret; and a by-name might not be recognised at all by the person it was applied to, unless they adopted it or gave it themselves. A list of four kinds cannot say any of that, which is why this page does not present one.
